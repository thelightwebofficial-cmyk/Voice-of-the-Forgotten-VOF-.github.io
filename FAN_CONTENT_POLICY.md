# Voice of the Forgotten (VOF) Fan Content Policy v1.0

**Last Updated:** August 2026

First of all, **thank you** for supporting **Voice of the Forgotten (VOF)**! ❤️

Whether you're making memes, fan art, edits, AMVs, theories, or reviews, your creativity helps the community grow. This policy explains what fan creations are welcome and what isn't.

This policy applies to **Voice of the Forgotten (VOF)**, its story, characters, artwork, world, logos, and related materials created by **Light** under **Light Web Official**.

# 💰 Commercial Use

Fan content created under this policy is **for non-commercial purposes only**.

You may **not**:

* Sell fan art using official VOF assets.
* Sell merchandise using VOF characters or logos.
* Publish paid translations.
* Monetize copies of the novel.
* Create NFTs using VOF content.
* License VOF content to others.

If you'd like to collaborate commercially, please contact Light Web Official first.

---

# 🚫 What You May NOT Do

Please do **not**:

* Upload the novel or chapters elsewhere.
* Recreate the official reading experience.
* Publish modified versions of the story.
* Translate and publish the novel.
* Claim VOF as your own work.
* Remove copyright notices.
* Mislead others into believing your work is official.
* Use official artwork as your own creation without permission.
* Use VOF to promote hate, harassment, discrimination, illegal activity, or harmful content.
* Use VOF content to train AI models or datasets without written permission.

---

# 🤖 AI-Generated Fan Content

AI-generated fan creations are welcome.

If your work uses AI, please clearly mention:

* The AI tool or model used.
* Your custom AI model (if applicable).
* That the work is fan-created.

Example:

> Fan art created with [AI Tool Name].
> Based on Voice of the Forgotten by Light.

---

# 🛡️ Respect the Community

Please create fan content responsibly.

Do not use Voice of the Forgotten to spread:

* Hate speech
* Harassment
* Bullying
* Discrimination
* Illegal content
* Explicit sexual content
* Content involving the exploitation or abuse of minors
* Graphic or extreme violence intended to shock

Follow all Community Guidelines.
