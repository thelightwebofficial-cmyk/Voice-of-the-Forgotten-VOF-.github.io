# Voice of the Forgotten — Privacy Policy v1.0

**Last Updated: September 2026**

Voice of the Forgotten (VOF) is a public creative project by Light under Light Web Official.

## Information We Collect

The VOF field-copy website does not require an account and does not intentionally collect personal information through the reading page.

The site may rely on third-party services such as GitHub Pages, Google Docs, Webnovel and Instagram. Those services may process technical or account information according to their own privacy policies.

## Contact

If you contact Light through Instagram or another linked service, information you provide is handled by that service and may also be visible to Light for the purpose of responding.

Please do not send passwords, financial information, home addresses, or other sensitive information through public comments or messages.

## Cookies and Local Storage

The field-copy site may use browser local storage to remember a visitor's light/dark theme preference. This information stays in the visitor's browser and is not used to identify them.

## Third-Party Services

The site may embed or link to third-party services. Their own privacy policies and terms apply when you interact with those services.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the website or its services change. The latest version will be published on the official VOF website.
