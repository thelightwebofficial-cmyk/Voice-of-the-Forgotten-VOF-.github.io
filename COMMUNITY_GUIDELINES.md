# Voice of the Forgotten — Community Guidelines v1.0

**Last Updated: September 2026**

The VOF community is a place for readers, artists, writers and fans to discuss the story and create around it.

## Be Respectful

Treat other readers and creators with respect. Debate ideas without attacking people.

## No Harassment

Do not threaten, bully, stalk, impersonate, or repeatedly target another person.

## No Hate or Discrimination

Do not use VOF spaces to promote hatred or discrimination against people or groups.

## Keep Discussions Relevant

Keep comments, theories, reviews and fan discussions connected to Voice of the Forgotten and its community.

## Protect Spoilers

When discussing unreleased chapters or major story events, use an appropriate spoiler warning where the platform allows it.

## Respect Intellectual Property

Do not repost chapters or substantial portions of the novel, claim VOF material as your own, or remove creator credit. Fan creations are governed by the Fan Content Policy.

## AI and Fan Creations

Fan-created work is welcome when it follows the Fan Content Policy. Do not use VOF material to train AI models or datasets without written permission.

## Enforcement

Content or accounts may be restricted or removed when they violate these guidelines, the VOF License, or applicable law.
